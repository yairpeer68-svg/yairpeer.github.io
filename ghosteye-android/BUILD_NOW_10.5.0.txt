Ghost Eye Phone 10.5.0 — Windows build

1. Extract the ZIP to a new folder.
2. Open CMD inside the ghost-eye-phone folder.
3. Run:

BUILD_PHONE_WINDOWS.bat

Expected APK:
app\build\outputs\apk\debug\app-debug.apk

Requirements:
- Temurin JDK 21 installed under C:\Program Files\Eclipse Adoptium\jdk-21*
- Android SDK with platform 35 and Build Tools 34+
- Internet may be needed on the first Gradle build to obtain dependencies.

The app uses only two main tabs: קובץ and דומיין.
